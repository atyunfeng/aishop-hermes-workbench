# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain professional, welcoming and useful. Be respectful,
assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people, and make room for people
with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or repeatedly disrupting technical discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issues and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider
context, severity, repetition and the safety of affected people.

For a confidential report, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting flow
and mark the report as a code-of-conduct matter rather than a vulnerability.
Do not publish personal or sensitive evidence in a public issue.
